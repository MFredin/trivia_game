activity feed, achievements, and a feature that lets players submit their own trivia questions

affect any leaderboard, until approved.

- **You keep ownership of what you write.** By submitting a question, you grant us a

  submission (and any admin-edited version of it, such as a corrected difficulty tier) in the
  Service's live question bank if it is approved, for as long as the Service operates.
- You confirm that a question you submit is your own original wording (not copied from
  another source) and that, to your knowledge, the factual content is accurate.
- We may edit a submitted question for clarity, formatting, or difficulty calibration before
  approval, and may reject, hold, or remove any submission at our sole discretion — including

- We do not pay for submitted questions.
