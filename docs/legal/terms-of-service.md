## 1. What this service is

The Restricted Section ("the Service," "we," "us") is a free-to-play, browser-based trivia
game built around Harry Potter–themed trivia questions. It includes solo quiz modes, a daily
challenge, real-time head-to-head "duels" against other players, leaderboards, achievements, and
a feature that lets players submit their own trivia questions for review. It also has a social
side: player profiles (an avatar and a short bio), a member directory, friends, an activity feed,
the ability to block and report other players, and **Owl Post**, short private messages between players (anyone by default, or friends only, or off, as each player chooses).

**The Service is an unofficial fan project.** As stated in the app itself:

## 4. Profiles, Owl Post, and what you share

**Your profile.** Your username, avatar, bio, favourite book and subject, pinned achievements,
house, and your game statistics and achievements are visible to any signed-in player, and the
member directory is open. Who can see your friends list is your choice (friends only by default).
The avatar is drawn from fixed options; nothing can be uploaded.

**Blocking.** You can block any player. A block ends any friendship between you, and after it
neither of you can find, see, message, or challenge the other. They are not told. You can undo a
block in Settings.

**Owl Post.** Owl Post lets players send each other short plain-text messages, with an optional
subject. By default any player can send you one; you can limit it to friends or switch it off in Settings,
and someone who is not your friend can send only one until you answer. You can block or report anyone
who writes to you. Please understand how it works:

- It is **not** end-to-end encrypted and **not** a place for anything you would want to keep
  secret. We do not read messages as a matter of course, and moderators cannot browse anyone's
  inbox.
- **If a player reports a conversation and chooses to include the recent messages, the last 20
  messages of that conversation — including the ones you wrote — are copied into the report and
  shown to moderators.** By using Owl Post you accept that this can happen.
- We may also look at messages where a serious safety concern or a legal obligation requires it.
- Messages are deleted automatically after **90 days**. "Delete for me" hides a message from you
  only; the other person keeps their copy until the 90 days are up.
- You can switch Owl Post off in Settings. If you do, you cannot send or receive. A moderator may
  also stop you sending ("mute") for a period.
- When an account is deleted, its messages (sent and received) are deleted too.

**What you write is yours, with a license to us.** For your bio and anything else you write in the
Service (other than a suggested question, see Section 6), you keep ownership but give us a
non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to store and display it in the Service for as long
as it is there. 🚩 *Attorney: confirm.*

## 5. Moderation, suspension and termination

Anyone can report a player, for an offensive name or bio, harassment, impersonation, cheating, or
another reason. The player reported is **not** told who reported them. Reports are reviewed by a
person, who decides what, if anything, to do. Depending on the situation, we may:

- send you a **warning** that you must acknowledge before continuing;
- require you to **change your username**;
- **clear your bio** or **reset your avatar**;
- **hold your scores off the leaderboards**;
- **mute** you (stop you sending Owl Post) for a period;
- **suspend** your account for a period, or **ban** it until we lift the ban;
- or, for a serious or repeated violation, skip steps.

Whenever we do this we tell you what we did and why, in a note shown to you. We may also suspend
or terminate an account, with or without notice, for suspected cheating flagged by our anti-cheat
system, for abusive conduct, or for any other reason we consider sufficient to protect the Service
or its players. We do not use automatic sanctions: a person decides. 🚩 *There is no formal appeal
process beyond the in-app Submit Feedback form; decide whether one is needed.*

**If you are banned,** we keep a one-way hash of your email address so the ban cannot be avoided
by registering again, even if you delete your account. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).

**Leaving.** You can stop using the Service at any time, and you can **delete your account in the
app** (Settings → Delete account). Deleting removes your name, email, password, avatar, bio,
friends, blocks, achievements, activity and Owl Post messages; your game runs and scores remain
without your name, shown as "Deleted player." Records of reports and moderation actions are kept,
linked only to the anonymised account. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md), Section 7.

The Service lets players suggest trivia questions through a submission screen. Submitted
questions are held for admin review and do not appear in the live question bank, and do not
affect any leaderboard, until approved.

- **You keep ownership of what you write.** By submitting a question, you grant us a

  submission (and any admin-edited version of it, such as a corrected difficulty tier) in the
  Service's live question bank if it is approved, for as long as the Service operates.
- You confirm that a question you submit is your own original wording (not copied from
  another source) and that, to your knowledge, the factual content is accurate.
- We may edit a submitted question for clarity, formatting, or difficulty calibration before
  approval, and may reject, hold, or remove any submission at our sole discretion — including
  one already approved, if we later determine it is inaccurate, duplicated, or otherwise
  unsuitable. Rejection does not require an explanation, though reviewers may leave one.
- We do not pay for submitted questions.
- A question you submitted stays in the question bank, without your name, if you delete your account.
